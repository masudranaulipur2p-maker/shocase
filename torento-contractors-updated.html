A few helpful answers

Before we
get started.

Every project is different. Let’s talk through your plans and what’s possible for your home.

What kind of projects do you take on?

We work on custom homes, whole-home renovations and residential additions. Share your plans and location and we’ll let you know if we’re the right fit.

When should we get in touch?

Reach out as early as you like—even if you’re still gathering ideas. A good early conversation can help you understand the steps ahead.

How do you keep a project on track?

We agree on scope, schedule and communication at the start. You’ll have a consistent point of contact throughout the build.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Toronto and select Greater Toronto Area communities. Tell us where your project is when you get in touch.
